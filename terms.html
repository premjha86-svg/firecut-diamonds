References to 15-minute or 30-minute responses and 2-working-day or 7-working-day delivery are target timings, not unconditional guarantees. They depend on business hours, enquiry completeness, availability, manufacturing requirements and logistics.

5. Certification and grading

GIA, IGI, HRD Antwerp and other laboratory names are referenced for educational/contextual purposes. A grading report is not implied unless it is actually supplied for the particular stone. Any report should be checked against the actual item and report details.

6. BIS hallmarking

BIS hallmarking concerns applicable precious-metal articles, not a blanket certification of diamonds. For eligible jewellery, the applicable hallmark and HUID details should be verified on the actual article. FireCut does not represent a jewellery item as BIS-hallmarked unless that claim is supported by the item and applicable documentation.

Some website visuals may be illustrative or digitally created. They are not proof that the exact pictured stone or jewellery is in stock or identical to an item later quoted.
