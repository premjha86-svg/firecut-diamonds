02 · The promise

We don't compete
only on price.

We would rather lose a sale than compromise the standard of the diamond we put in front of you.

“I don't guarantee that I am the lowest.
I guarantee that I will not compromise on quality.”

That is the Firecut difference. Diamond pricing changes with shape, size, color, clarity, cut, fluorescence, proportions, certification, growth method and the exact stone available. So we will never make a blanket promise that we are always the cheapest.

Our promise is more important: when we recommend a diamond for your requirement, we want its quality to be better than what you are being shown elsewhere for the same requirement. We select with the intention of giving you the best quality we can source — not simply the lowest number on a quotation.
