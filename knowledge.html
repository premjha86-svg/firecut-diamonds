FIRST THING TO KNOW

Natural and lab-grown are both real diamonds.

The key difference is origin. A natural diamond forms deep inside the Earth over a very long geological time. A lab-grown diamond is grown by people using advanced technology that recreates diamond-forming conditions.

THE 4Cs + OUR 5TH C

Think of choosing a diamond like choosing a great photo.

You don’t judge a photo only by its file size. You look at focus, colour, clarity and overall impact. Diamonds work in a similar way.

C

Cut

How well does it play with light?
Cut is about proportions, symmetry and finish. A beautifully cut diamond can look lively, bright and full of sparkle.

C

Colour

How much body colour can you see?
For classic white diamonds, grading generally moves from colourless toward more visible warmth. Fancy colours are a different and exciting world.

C

Clarity

What is happening inside?
Natural and lab-grown diamonds can contain tiny internal features called inclusions and surface features called blemishes. Their visibility matters more than memorising a grade.

C

Carat

How much does the diamond weigh?
Carat is weight, not a direct measurement of visual size. Two diamonds with the same carat can look different depending on shape and proportions.

5C

Craftsmanship

How beautifully was everything brought together?
Our fifth C is the human touch: stone selection, setting, finishing, proportion, comfort and the final jewellery experience.

LAB-GROWN DIAMONDS

CVD vs HPHT — explained like you’re 10.

Imagine two chefs making the same chocolate cake. The ingredients are still cake ingredients; the kitchen setup and method are different.

CVD

Chemical Vapour Deposition

Think of a very controlled chamber filled with carbon-containing gas. Under the right conditions, carbon builds up layer by layer on a tiny diamond seed.

  • Growth happens in a chamber
  • Uses carbon-rich gases and controlled energy
  • Often produces flat/plate-like growth before finishing
HPHT

High Pressure High Temperature

Think of a tiny artificial Earth experiment. Very high pressure and high temperature encourage carbon to crystallise into diamond around a seed.

  • Recreates diamond-forming pressure & temperature
  • Uses a diamond seed and carbon source
  • Different growth conditions can leave different growth characteristics
THE SIMPLE ANSWERBoth CVD and HPHT can produce genuine diamond.

The right choice depends on the stone, quality, grading, intended jewellery, budget and your preferences. Don’t choose only by the three letters.
