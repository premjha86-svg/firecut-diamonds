02 · What should I write?

From a full spec to just the basics.

If you know exactly what you want, send the full specification. If not, do not worry — carat, shape, size, certificate and origin are enough to start.

Example: detailed

You can copy a requirement like this:

1.00 carat Round – E – VS2 – GIA – 3EX – None – No BGM – No Black

This gives us a much clearer target. We can then look for the right stone instead of wasting time on irrelevant options.

Example: simple

Do not know all the grades? Simply send:

1.00 carat · Round · GIA · Natural diamond

or

2.00 carat · Oval · Lab-grown diamond

We can take it from there and guide you on the quality, available options and price.
