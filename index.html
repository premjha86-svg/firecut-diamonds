Certification

Graded by labs
you can look up.

Certified stones come with grading papers from independent, internationally recognised laboratories — not an in-house guess.

Gemological Institute of America
International Gemological Institute
HRD Antwerp (Hoge Raad voor Diamant)
Gemological Science International
Solitaire Gemological Laboratories

These are Firecut-designed seals identifying each laboratory by name — not the labs' own trademarked logos, which they reserve for registered retail partners. Which lab applies depends on the specific stone and is confirmed with you on WhatsApp before anything is finalised.
