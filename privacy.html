Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Who this notice is for

This notice explains how FireCut Diamonds & Jewellery handles personal information shared through this website or during an enquiry. It is intended to be read in clear language and should be adapted to the final legal entity, vendors and operational processes before launch.

2. Information you may choose to share

Depending on your enquiry, this may include your name, phone number, email address, WhatsApp messages, diamond/jewellery preferences, budget, delivery requirements, design references and other information you voluntarily provide.

3. Why we use it

We use enquiry information to respond to your request, prepare or communicate relevant options, arrange requested services, communicate about an enquiry, maintain necessary business records, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations where applicable.

4. Data minimisation

We aim to request only information reasonably needed for the enquiry or service. Do not send passwords, OTPs, banking PINs, card authentication information or other unnecessary confidential credentials.

5. WhatsApp

If you choose the WhatsApp button, you are choosing to communicate through WhatsApp. The information you send is processed through WhatsApp and is also subject to WhatsApp's own policies. FireCut does not control WhatsApp's systems.

6. Sharing

Relevant information may be shared with service providers or partners where reasonably necessary to fulfil the service you requested, such as grading/certification, manufacturing or logistics, or where required by law. We do not sell enquiry information as a business practice.

7. Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably required for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate business records and applicable legal requirements. Appropriate practical safeguards should be maintained for systems and devices used to handle enquiries.

8. Your choices

Subject to applicable law, you may contact us to ask what personal information we hold, request correction, raise a concern, or ask about deletion/withdrawal where applicable. Contact support@firecutdiamond.com.

9. Cookies and analytics

The current site does not require an online account or checkout. If analytics, advertising pixels, cookies or other tracking technologies are added later, the privacy notice and consent/notice mechanism should be updated before those tools are activated.

10. Applicable privacy law

FireCut will review its data practices against applicable Indian privacy and data-protection requirements, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, as and when the relevant provisions apply to the business. The exact notice, consent and rights workflow should be aligned with the final business setup and implementation timeline.
