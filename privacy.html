Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you contact FireCut, we may receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address, WhatsApp messages, jewellery/diamond preferences, budget and delivery requirements.

Why we use it

We use enquiry information only to respond to your request, prepare suitable options, communicate with you, improve our service and maintain records needed for legitimate business and legal purposes.

What we do not do

This website does not operate an online shopping cart, checkout or payment gateway. We do not ask for card details on this website. We do not sell your enquiry information to unrelated third parties.

WhatsApp

If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, your conversation is handled through WhatsApp and is also subject to WhatsApp’s own privacy terms. We only receive the information you send to us.

Sharing

We may share relevant enquiry details with trusted service providers or partners only when reasonably necessary to fulfil your requested diamond/jewellery consultation, certification, logistics or related service, or when required by law.

Cookies & analytics

Future versions of the website may use basic analytics or cookies to understand website usage. Any such tools should be configured to follow applicable privacy requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, request correction, or ask us to stop using information where applicable. Contact support@firecutdiamond.com.
